# Unit blog

Only when a lesson is worth preserving. Ask “essay or skip?”

Invent nothing. Sources: spec, catalog, gates, WORKING_STATE, diff.

Write `docs/journal/unit-<slug>.md`: what we thought, why it was harder,
mistakes, tradeoffs, what future-you must remember, concepts unlocked.

~400–900 words. Propose a commit subject. Do not commit.
