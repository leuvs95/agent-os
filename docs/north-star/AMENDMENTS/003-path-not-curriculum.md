# Amendment 003 — path-not-curriculum

**Status:** accepted
**Type:** both
**From:** constitution v1.0 / ladder v1.2
**To:** constitution v1.1 / ladder v1.3
**Date:** 2026-08-27
**Evidence:** TaxiLabs CI followed Agent OS in good faith. Kernel law 5
orders units by risk retired and slices by contract. That produced
locally rational rows: real risks, real contracts, not obviously
layer-sliced. Destination pressure was not inevitable, so the owner
had to intervene to protect the accepted product path. That
intervention is the skip that costs something. It is **not** a skip
of `--range`, hooks, or CI, and it does **not** thaw rows 4–7.

## Why

Via negativa currently produces a **risk curriculum**. We want it to
produce a **path**.

The ladder is the minimum risk-retiring path from uncertainty to an
accepted observable product outcome. Risk discovery is not
implementation authorization. A discovered risk has not earned code.

Only a risk that threatens the accepted Observable Product Outcome,
cannot be retired by a cheaper valid treatment, and requires product
behavior may become a builder row.

**Research broadly. Build narrowly. Every unit must earn its place on
the shortest trustworthy path to the accepted product outcome.**

This is a new kernel row **8**, not a thaw of 4–7. Amendment 002’s
thaw probe (“when 003 opens, name the product skip and which frozen
row that cost requires”) does not fire: the demonstrated cost is a
missing destination law, not a skipped checker invocation. Thaw of
4–7 remains a later amendment (004+), still requiring a skip of
`--range` / invocation that costs something, or a second importer
with that skip.
